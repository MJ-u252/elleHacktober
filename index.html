Forensic Meteorologist Assessment

Observed Data: On the day of the incident (Oct 31), the area recorded absolutely zero precipitation, mild to chilly temperatures dropping below freezing (23.9°F min), and gentle winds (max 7.9 mph). The following day (Nov 1) saw trace precipitation (0.02") and higher winds.

Reasonable Implications: The zero precipitation reading on Oct 31 strongly challenges any claims of heavy rain or naturally occurring deep mud *during* the Halloween gathering.

Unresolved Questions: As these are daily aggregates, they cannot rule out highly localized micro-events, indoor/outdoor plumbing leaks, spilled drinks, or condensation that could cause a localized slippery surface. Furthermore, weather data cannot definitively prove an alibi or identify a perpetrator.
